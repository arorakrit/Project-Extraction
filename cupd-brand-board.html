Brand Direction — v0.1 · Internal
Cup’d

The log for people who take the cup seriously.

A community record of specialty coffee — what you drank, where you drank it, and whether it was worth remembering. Built around the ritual of cupping, not the chore of reviewing.

The Brand Idea

Every great coffee gets cupped — tasted, judged, talked about, remembered. Cup’d turns that ritual into a record, and the record into a community. You weren’t just there. You cupped it.

Taste

Every log builds your palate — the private taste graph that becomes the platform’s moat.

Place

You cup somewhere real. The venue check-in is the wedge and the daily habit.

Together

Cupping is shared by nature. See who else cupped what you cupped, where.

Jade
#3DBE8B
Signature accent. Green-bean, origin, growth. Use sparingly.
Bone
#E9E7DD
Text + data surfaces. Cool oat — deliberately NOT warm cream.

The fork — accent personality. Jade (cool, origin-led) is the clean break from your warm personal brand. The alternative is a clay/ember accent (#C8643C) — warmer, more “the cup itself,” more inviting to casuals — but it edges toward saffron territory and risks bleeding into your personal identity. Recommendation: hold jade.

The Surface That Proves It

One card carries all three pillars.

Place the venue is the anchor — Prufrock, Shoreditch.

Taste rating + flavour tags feed the palate graph.

Together “3 others cupped this here today” — the line that turns a private log into a community. This is the moment the brand stops being Untappd-for-coffee and becomes a place you belong.
